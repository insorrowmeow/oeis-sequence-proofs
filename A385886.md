# Which rows of A385886 consist entirely of ones?

Row 7 of the OEIS triangle A385886 is $(1,1,1,1)$. Its published list of candidate row indices, A001911 minus 2, consists of numbers of the form $F_{k+3}-4$. For 7 to appear on the list, 11 would have to be a Fibonacci number. The consecutive Fibonacci numbers 8 and 13 rule this out. Counting the empty row as the first row gives ordinal position 8, which would require the Fibonacci number 12. [A385886, including row 7](https://oeis.org/A385886) · [A001911](https://oeis.org/A001911)

## Recovering row 7

Read the positions of the one-bits of a nonnegative integer from right to left. At a gap of one position, start a new group. At a larger gap, keep the next position in the same group. Record the sizes of the groups, keeping only the first occurrence of each resulting row as the integers increase. For example, 15 is binary 1111. All three gaps equal one, so all four groups have size one.

| First integer | Binary | Zero-based row | Group sizes |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 0 | empty |
| 1 | 1 | 1 | (1) |
| 3 | 11 | 2 | (1,1) |
| 5 | 101 | 3 | (2) |
| 7 | 111 | 4 | (1,1,1) |
| 11 | 1011 | 5 | (1,2) |
| 13 | 1101 | 6 | (2,1) |
| 15 | 1111 | 7 | (1,1,1,1) |

## The corrected indices

For a specified nonempty row, its least integer representative puts the lowest one at bit 0, uses a gap of 1 between groups and a gap of 2 within a group. Any larger allowable gap moves at least one one-bit farther left. Thus the least representatives are exactly the binary words beginning and ending in 1 that avoid 00. Their number at length $d$ is $F_d$, with $F_0=0,F_1=1$. The counts at lengths 1 and 2 are both one. At length at least 3, remove the leading 1 from a word beginning in 11, or the leading 10 from a word beginning in 101. This gives the Fibonacci recurrence.

The all-ones word of length $d$ is the last word at its length. Including the initial empty row at index zero, its row index is $F_1+\cdots+F_d=F_{d+2}-1$. The nonempty rows consisting entirely of ones therefore occur at indices **1, 2, 4, 7, 12, 20, …**. [General classification and related identities](proofs/early/composition-rank-and-wythoff.txt)

## Lean verification

`AntiRunWitness.counterexample` checks the bits of the integers through 15, removes repeated earlier rows, and excludes 7 and 8 from the proposed list for every natural index. It uses Lean 4.19.0 and the standard library. The general formula above is proved in the written argument. From the repository root, run `lake env lean AntiRunWitness.lean`. This work used AI assistance. [Lean source](AntiRunWitness.lean)
