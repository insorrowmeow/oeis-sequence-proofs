# A coefficient proof of both algorithms for A208832

In [MathOverflow 482170](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/482170/algorithm-for-frac11-x-sum-limits-n-0-inftyanxn-prod-limits-k) the proposed sequential vector sweep and the proposed two-index recurrence both compute the coefficients in [Paul Hanna's A208832](https://oeis.org/A208832). The entry starts at index zero. The vector begins its output at index one.

Put fᵢ=(1−ix)/(1+ix), and Bⱼ=xʲ f₁⋯fⱼ, with B₀=1. Since Bⱼ begins with xʲ, the coefficients in any expansion in this basis are unique by successive comparison of lowest powers.

Start with V₀=1/(1−x), so the target series equals 1+xV₀. For i≥1 set aᵢ=Vᵢ₋₁(0) and define Vᵢ by

    (1+ix)/(1−ix) Vᵢ₋₁ = aᵢ + x Vᵢ.

This defines an ordinary formal series because the constant terms agree. Replacing xBᵢ₋₁Vᵢ₋₁ by Bᵢ(aᵢ+xVᵢ) shows inductively that the target equals Σⱼ₌₀ⁱ aⱼBⱼ + xBᵢVᵢ, with a₀=1. The remainder has order at least i+1. Thus the aᵢ just constructed are precisely A208832.

Write vⱼ=[xʲ]Vᵢ₋₁. Since (1+ix)/(1−ix)=1+2Σₕ≥₁ iʰxʰ, we obtain

    [xʲ]Vᵢ = vⱼ₊₁ + 2 Σₕ₌₀ʲ i^(j+1−h) vₕ.

The initial row consists of ones. Therefore the entire R(i,j) in the question is [xʲ]Vᵢ and R(i,0)=aᵢ₊₁.

For the in-place algorithm, use zero-based cell numbers j. At the start of the j-th update the current cell contains cⱼ, where c₀=v₀ and cⱼ₊₁=i cⱼ+vⱼ₊₁. The first assignment computes cⱼ₊₁ in the next cell. The second writes i cⱼ+cⱼ₊₁ into the current one. Expanding cⱼ=Σₕ₌₀ʲ i^(j−h)vₕ gives the displayed formula. Only the current cell and its right neighbor are read, so a vector of length m suffices for m−1 shrinking sweeps. Its initial output is a₁ and its i-th sweep supplies aᵢ₊₁, including the case m=1.

[Lean verification](RationalSweep.lean) checks the sequential update, its closed convolution, and the coefficient identity of the rational multiplier for every old row and index. The formal-series expansion and uniqueness argument above complete the identification. No priority claim is made.
