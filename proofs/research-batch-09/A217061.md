# Factorial sweep for A217061

Both the vector algorithm and the recurrence R in [MathOverflow 481279](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/481279/efficient-algorithm-for-a217061) compute the intended sequence for every positive output index. There is a typographical discrepancy in the introductory fourfold sum: the displayed exponent ℓ−j differs from j−ℓ in the author's PARI code and in [A217061](https://oeis.org/A217061). Already at n=2 the displayed sum is 3/2. Its two nonzero contributions are 1 from m=2 and 1/2 from (m,k,j,ℓ)=(1,1,1,0). The program, the recurrence, and OEIS all give 3.

### One pass of the algorithm

Number the pre-pass cells v₀,v₁,… starting at zero. Just before processing position j its working value is cⱼ, with c₀=v₀. The next cell still has its old value vⱼ₊₁. The two assignments first set cⱼ₊₁=(j+1)cⱼ+vⱼ₊₁ and then write wⱼ=(j+1)cⱼ+cⱼ₊₁. Division of the carry recurrence by (j+1)! telescopes, giving

    wⱼ = vⱼ₊₁ + 2(j+1)! Σ_{h=0}^j vₕ/h!.

This is exactly one row update of R, from the all-ones initial row. The last surviving cell after each pass depends only on entries present in the finite vector: output position j reads through old position j+1. Thus after i passes the leftmost cell equals R(i,0) whenever the initial length exceeds i.

### Identification for every index

For a row v put F(y)=Σ vⱼ yʲ/j!, set H(y)=(1+y)/(1−y), and write D=d/dy. Multiplication by H=1+2y/(1−y) followed by differentiation gives the exponential generating function of w, coefficient by coefficient using the formula above. Consequently the row after i passes has series (D H)ⁱ exp(y), with H denoting multiplication.

Let Y(x) be the compositional inverse of 2 log(1+y)−y. This is the generating series of [A006351](https://oeis.org/A006351), and by definition exp(Y(x)) is the exponential generating series of A217061. Differentiating the inverse identity gives Y'=H(Y) and Y(0)=0. The chain rule gives the (i+1)-st derivative of exp(Y) at zero as [(H D)ⁱ⁺¹ exp(y)] at y=0. Since D exp=exp, associativity gives D(HD)ⁱ exp=(DH)ⁱ exp, and H(0)=1 allows the leading H to be removed at evaluation. Therefore this derivative equals R(i,0), proving R(i,0)=A217061(i+1). The original vector outputs start with a(1)=1; the separate a(0)=1 comes directly from the generating series.

[Lean 4.19.0](FactorialSweep.lean) proves the normalized carry formula, the general row update and the operator iteration identity. The inverse-series and chain-rule identification is a written proof. No first-publication claim is made.
