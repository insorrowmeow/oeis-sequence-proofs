# Positive terms of A260711, their unique witnesses, and the permutation A120242

On February 29, 2024, user231922 asked for proofs of formulas connecting odd square differences, Proth numbers, binary palindromes and a permutation of Leroy Quet. All the formulas in that question hold for every positive index. The public question displayed no answers when checked. There is prior literature on the spacing of the Proth numbers, including Lemma 2.2 of Borsos, Kovács and Tihanyi. The claim here is a solution of the linked question. [MathOverflow 466152](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/466152/formula-and-smallest-solution-for-the-a260711) · [OEIS A260711](https://oeis.org/A260711) · [OEIS A080075](https://oeis.org/A080075) · [Borsos, Kovács and Tihanyi](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-021-00536-2)

## From squares to Proth numbers

Let $a(n)$ denote the $n$th positive term of A260711, so $a(1)=8$. Take positive odd integers $x>y$ with $x+y$ a power of two. Write $t=(x+y)/2=2^m$. Since $x+y\ge4$, we have $m\ge1$. Now $z=(x-y)/2=t-y$ is odd and $0<z<2^m$. Thus

$$x=2^m+z,\qquad y=2^m-z,\qquad x^2-y^2=4w,\qquad w=2^m z.$$

Every pair $(m,z)$ satisfying these bounds gives a valid witness. For a given $w$, its unique decomposition into an odd factor and a power of two determines $m$ and $z$, hence determines $x$ and $y$. The possible values of $w+1$ are exactly the Proth numbers in increasing order. Therefore $a(n)=4(\mathrm{A080075}(n)-1)$, and the two parameters $(z,m)$ in their increasing order of $w$ are A157892 and A157893. [A157892](https://oeis.org/A157892) · [A157893](https://oeis.org/A157893)

## The sorted blocks

Fix $e\ge1$. In the interval $2^e\le w<2^{e+1}$ write $w=2^m z$ with $z$ odd. The defining inequality $z<2^m$ is equivalent to $w<2^{2m}$, or $e<2m$. Thus the possible values of $w$ are exactly the multiples of $2^{\lfloor e/2\rfloor+1}$ in this interval.

Write $e=2L-1+j$, with $L\ge1$ and $j\in\{0,1\}$, and put $H=2^{L-1}$. The values of $w$ in this interval, in order, are

$$2^{L+j}(H+r),\qquad 0\le r<H.$$

For each smaller $L'$ there are $2\cdot2^{L'-1}$ values, one row for each $j$. Their total is $2H-2$. Consequently the one-based index $n$ of the displayed value satisfies $N=n+1=(2+j)H+r$.

Conversely, for any $n\ge1$, set $L=\lfloor\log_2 N\rfloor$, $H=2^{L-1}$, $j=[N\ge3H]$, and $r=N\bmod H$. Then $N=(2+j)H+r$, so the preceding block supplies the term of the required rank.

## The formulas in the question

For each length $2L-1$ and $2L$, an increasing binary palindrome is determined by its first $L$ bits. Its first bit must be one, and the prefixes run in increasing numerical order from $H$ to $2H-1$. Each length therefore contributes the same $H$ prefixes, and the lengths come in exactly the blocks just counted. The prefix of the $n$th positive binary palindrome is $c(n)=H+r$. [A162751](https://oeis.org/A162751)

Write $v=v_2(c(n))$. Since $w=2^{L+j}c(n)$, its two Proth parameters are

$$b_1(n)=c(n)/2^v,\qquad b_2(n)=L+j+v.$$

When $2H\le N<3H$, we have $H\le\lfloor 2N/3\rfloor<2H$. When $3H\le N<4H$, we have $2H\le\lfloor 2N/3\rfloor<4H$. Hence $\lfloor\log_2\lfloor 2N/3\rfloor\rfloor=L-1+j$. Multiplying $w$ by four gives the requested formula

$$a(n)=c(n)\,2^{\lfloor\log_2\lfloor 2(n+1)/3\rfloor\rfloor+3}.$$

The unique witness from the first section is $x_n=2^{b_2(n)}+b_1(n)$, $y_n=2^{b_2(n)}-b_1(n)$.

## The additional conjecture about A120242

For a positive integer $t$, write $t=2^{m-1}+q$ with $m\ge1$ and $0\le q<2^{m-1}$. Define $z=2q+1$ and $w(t)=2^mz$. This parametrizes the admissible $w$ bijectively. Moreover, $t=\lfloor(2^m+z)/2\rfloor=\lfloor x/2\rfloor$. Let $P(t)$ be the one-based rank of $w(t)$ in the sorted list of admissible values. We prove that $P$ is A120241. [Definition of A120241](https://oeis.org/A120241) · [Inverse permutation A120242](https://oeis.org/A120242)

After $P(1),\ldots,P(t-1)$ have been used, compare $w(t)$ with the values $w(t')$ still available for $t'\ge t$. For the same exponent $m$, later values have a larger odd factor. For an exponent $m+d$ with $d\ge1$, a smaller value must have the form $2^{m+d}z'$ with $2^d z'<2q+1$ and $z'$ positive odd. Each such choice is automatically admissible because $z'<2^m<2^{m+d}$. Unique odd-part factorization puts these choices in bijection with $2,4,\ldots,2q$. Exactly $q$ remaining ranks precede $P(t)$.

Thus $P(t)$ is the $(q+1)$st unused positive integer. Since $q=t-2^{\lfloor\log_2 t\rfloor}$, this is precisely the recursive definition of A120241, including $t=1$. Inverting gives $\mathrm{A120242}(n)=\lfloor x_n/2\rfloor$.

## Formal verification

[A260711.lean](A260711.lean) uses Lean 4.19.0 and mathlib. It proves the two-way passage between odd square witnesses and their Proth parameters, uniqueness for every positive difference, and the divisibility criterion on every interval between consecutive powers of four. It also checks the explicit witnesses for every block and both floor-log evaluations in the index formula. The count establishing the one-based indices, the binary-palindrome interpretation and the inverse-permutation argument above are written proofs.
